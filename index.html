Front suspension kinematics and wheel rate

Team Hadron car · 230 kg · 45/55 · 1530 mm wheelbase · 1290 mm front track

  • Built the front suspension subsystem in MSC Adams/Car for the Team Hadron car.
  • Runs Parallel Wheel Travel to get camber, toe, roll centre height and wheel rate against wheel travel.
  • Studies the bellcrank motion ratio, because it sets how a 50 N/mm front spring becomes wheel rate and ride frequency (calculator below).
  • MSC Adams/Car

Wheel rate = spring rate × motion ratio². Ride frequency = √(wheel rate ÷ sprung mass) ÷ 2π, using 50 N/mm, 45% front weight and a 230 kg car. Tyre stiffness and anti-roll bar are ignored. The motion ratio and unsprung mass are sliders for exploring, not measured values.
